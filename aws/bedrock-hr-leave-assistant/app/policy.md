# Example Corp leave policy

## Annual leave

Every permanent employee gets 12 days of paid annual leave per calendar year after 12 months of service. Unused days do not carry over to the next year. Annual leave is counted in working days, so weekends are not deducted.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is paid and does not reduce annual leave. An absence longer than 2 consecutive days needs a doctor's note, submitted to HR within 3 working days after returning.

## Maternity leave

Female employees get 3 months of paid maternity leave: 1.5 months before and 1.5 months after giving birth.

## Paternity leave

Male employees get 2 paid days when their wife gives birth.

## Marriage leave

Employees get 3 paid days for their own wedding.

## Bereavement leave

Employees get 2 paid days when a spouse, child, parent, or parent-in-law passes away.

## How to request leave

Submit a request in this app at least 7 days before the first day of leave. HR approves or rejects it within 3 working days.
